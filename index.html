How IDF works

Agents execute. People decide at four moments.

The whole loop, from business outcome to confirmed result. Pick an execution model; only the Execution lane changes.

Agents split the scope, build and verify. Verification writes the review report for Fitness.

a named person decides work or record forward sent back signal returns

Step through it

  1. Leaders set directionStrategy and objectives become a business outcome with a signal.
  2. Discovery writes an IntentThe team turns customer signals and ideas into an outcome, a mechanism and a signal.
  3. DirectionThe outcome owner approves the intent and cycle scope, or sends it back.
  4. Execution runs an iterationThe work is built and tested, deployed switched off, and summarized in a review report.
  5. FitnessAn independent reviewer reads the report and uses the feature. No means a new iteration; three misses go back to Direction.
  6. ExposureOne named person turns the switch ON, or records why not.
  7. ConfirmationThe owner watches the signal. Not there yet means a new cycle, starting at Direction.
  8. The result feeds UpstreamCompleted or abandoned, the intent becomes evidence for the next business outcome.
